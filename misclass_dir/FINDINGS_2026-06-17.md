## Executive summary

We hunted for novel structure in COTA's 74 line-of-therapy over-splits (vs 706 aligned transitions, 136 patients) beyond the known facts (one-directional over-splitting, the line-number decay curve, subset/addition directions). The single most useful novel finding is **clinical**: over-splits cluster where the *previous* line ended with a **blank discontinuation reason** (the line-defining event is undocumented) — OR 3.24, robust to line number, transplant, and patient clustering, and crucially *orthogonal* to the known drug-drop pattern. The dominant *mechanism* by raw volume is the **identical-drug-set / bracket-phase artifact** (40% of over-splits, OR ~39): COTA opens a new line when only the regimen-string phase formatting changes. Most other "novel" leads (transplant swing drug, inverted de-escalation sub-curve, independent line-burden effect) were **weakened or refuted** by the verifiers once the right control or non-inflated x-axis was used — those are documented below so the professor knows what to stop chasing.

**Headline:** A blank prior-line discontinuation reason triples over-split odds (OR 3.24) and is a separate failure mode from the larger identical-drug-set parsing artifact (OR ~39).

## Ranked novel findings

**1. Over-splits follow an UNDOCUMENTED prior-line ending (blank discontinue_reason) — and attribution to the *previous* row is what reveals it.** [clinical]
- Prev-line blank reason: **24/74 (32.4%) OVER vs 91/706 (12.9%) ALIGN; Fisher OR=3.24 (bootstrap 95% CI 1.84–5.49), p=4.2e-5**, risk diff +19.5pp. Using the *current* row's reason instead gives a null (no-progression 44.6% vs 52.5%, OR 0.73, p=0.22) — the team's earlier base rate used the wrong row.
- Confidence **high**; verdict **confirmed**. Survives line stratification (L2–4 OR 3.21; L≥3 OR 2.75), is line-independent (mean line 5.13 vs 5.12), transplant-flat, spans 21 distinct patients, stable under first-token parsing.
- **Why it matters:** This is a genuinely new, actionable failure mode — COTA over-splits where there is *no coded clinical event* to anchor the line change, and the event belongs to the line that just ended. It is largely orthogonal to the known de-escalation pattern (only 25% of blank-prev over-splits are de-escalations vs 64% of documented-prev ones), so it is a *distinct* error class, not a relabeling.
- **Next step / viz:** Stacked bar of over-split vs aligned rate by prev-row reason (blank / progression / toxicity+preference), with the current-vs-previous attribution shown side-by-side to make the row-shift point visually.

**2. The identical-drug-set / bracket-phase formatting artifact is the dominant over-split mechanism by volume.** [data-artifact]
- Current row has an **identical parsed drug set to the prior row**: 30/74 (40.5%) OVER vs 12/706 (1.7%) ALIGN; **Fisher OR=39.4, RR=23.9, p=2.1e-24**. Survives line adjustment (logistic OR 25–39; line OR 0.92). Of the 30, **21 (70%) differ only in bracket-phase count** (a maintenance/continuation phase appears or drops) with no drug change.
- Confidence **high**; verdict **confirmed**. The steroid-churn hypothesis it grew out of is itself NULL (steroid-only changes = 2/74, 2.7%; dex-drop RR 1.06, p=0.86).
- **Why it matters:** This is the highest-yield single fix target and points at a concrete root cause — COTA's line logic is triggered by regimen-string *phase formatting* (bracket groups), not by an actual drug-therapy change. Partly extends the known `same_exact_regimen_repeated=20` label, but the ALIGN-control effect size, line-adjustment, and the bracket-phase mechanism (21/30) are new.
- **Next step / viz:** Among the 30 identical-set over-splits, show the distribution of prev→cur bracket-phase-count changes; pull 3 worked regimen-string examples for the appendix.

**3. PI+Steroids regimens are specifically PROTECTED against over-splitting — the only family combo surviving Bonferroni.** [statistical, borderline-clinical]
- Opportunity-normalized: **12/284 (4.2%) vs 62/496 (12.5%); Fisher OR=0.31, p=1.1e-4**. **Only 1 of 36 family combos (n_opp≥5) passes Bonferroni** (α=0.00139; next-smallest p=0.10). Survives line adjustment (Mantel-Haenszel OR 0.33) and exact-repeat adjustment (OR 0.40, LR p=0.006). PI-specific, not a generic steroid-doublet effect (IMiDs+Steroids OR 0.95, mAb+Steroids OR 1.29, both null).
- Confidence **medium→high**; verdict **confirmed**. The *assigned* "high-rate family" hypothesis is effectively NULL — every raw-rate leader (transplant/IMiD combos at 25–33%) had n≤10 and failed (all p>0.10).
- **Why it matters:** Inverts the framing — the interesting family signal is a regimen COTA handles *well* (stable PI+steroid maintenance/continuation it correctly does not re-line), and it is the one finding that survives strict multiplicity control. Mechanism partly runs through PI+Steroids rarely being an identical repeat (1.8%), so it overlaps Finding 2.
- **Next step / viz:** Forest plot of per-family over-split OR (opportunity-normalized) with Wilson CIs and the Bonferroni line drawn in, sorted by OR.

**4. Severe over-counting is pure repeat-offender accumulation, never a single big jump — Part 1 only.** [data-artifact/clinical]
- **Every one of the 74 over-splits is a per-transition jump of exactly +1** (cota_inc=+1, doc_inc=0, 74/74). Cumulative shifts of +2..+5 arise solely from a patient accumulating multiple separate +1 over-splits — per-patient max shift exactly equals the over-split count for all 52 offenders; one patient (b739c87a) supplies every +3/+4/+5. Repeat offenders have more *true* (doctor) lines (median 9 vs 6, p=0.008).
- Confidence **high** for Part 1; verdict **weakened** overall. The Part-2 claim ("high line burden *independently* raises the rate") is **refuted by endogeneity**: it used COTA's own line total (inflated by the very over-splits being predicted); swapping doctor ground-truth totals drops the logistic coef from +0.182 (p=4.6e-6) to +0.070 (p=0.16) and the early-line Fisher from OR 1.87/p=0.052 to OR ~1.4/p≈0.3.
- **Why it matters:** Establishes the clean "corrected-LOT = don't increment on doctor-hold rows" rule and tells the team that severity is *opportunity* (more transitions = more +1 chances), not a distinct super-spreader driver — pre-empting a tempting but circular "heavily-pretreated patients are error-prone on a count basis" claim.
- **Next step / viz:** Histogram of cumulative shift (+1..+5) with bars colored by number-of-distinct-over-split-rows, annotating the single 5× patient for chart review.

## Extensions to known patterns

- **Directionality identity (extends-known, confirmed, high).** COTA's LOT is literally a within-patient contiguous row index (cota seq = 1..n for all 136 patients; cota_inc≡+1; doc_inc∈{0,1}; crosstab exactly (0,1):74 and (1,1):706; binomial p=5.3e-23). Over-splitting is one-directional *by construction* — merges are unrepresentable. Novel useful corollary: `cota_minus_doctor_lot_shift = baseline_offset + running count of doctor-holds` (closed form, verified), and the lone "under-count" patient (ada4c8dc) is pure baseline left-censoring (offset −3 from row 1, every transition aligned), not a missed merge. Blind spot worth flagging: the over-split flag cannot detect baseline left-censoring offsets for mid-treatment entrants.
- **Decay curve is a real hazard, not survivorship, and is monotone (not quadratic) (extends-known, confirmed, high).** Per-transition hazard declines genuinely (Spearman ρ=−0.80, p=0.0096; logistic OR 0.849/line over L2–10). Verifier's decisive check: re-running on the **doctor's ground-truth line** gives OR=0.784, z=−2.86, p=0.004 — *steeper*, so the decay is NOT an x-axis artifact (I reproduced both: COTA OR 0.849 / doctor OR 0.784). The "quadratic/U-shape" is a tail artifact (3 events in risk sets of 2–4 at L14/L16); the quad LR p goes 0.034→0.63 when trimmed to L≤10. Power-law vs exponential indistinguishable (ΔAIC<0.3). L11+ "~0%" facts are survivorship-empty (≤24 patients reach L10).
- **Type dominates line; exact-repeat is flat and high-yield (extends-known, partially confirmed, high).** Transition *type* (exact-repeat/de-escalation/addition) carries the signal, not line number (χ²=263, ORs 109/26/8 vs line OR 0.98 after type control); exact-regimen-repeat over-splits at 71% (30/42) regardless of line. This is the robust core of the "curve-by-pattern" hypothesis and reinforces Finding 2.
- **Multi-phase PREVIOUS regimen at relapse (novel but confounded — keep narrow).** Prev-line multi-phase: relapsed (prev line≥2) over-split rate 19.7% vs 4.9% (OR 4.75, p=9.5e-8), and survives *removal of de-escalation rows* (relapsed non-de-escalation OR 4.08, p=4.8e-5 — reproduced). **However**, in the full sample its marginal effect (OR 2.71) collapses to **OR 1.12 once identical-drug-set is in the model**, and it is null among non-same-set transitions (OR 1.39, p=0.30 — reproduced). Resolution: prev-multiphase is mostly a *downstream correlate* of Finding 2 (multi-phase prior lines tend to be followed by a row repeating the induction drug set), with a residual, genuinely-independent effect surviving only in the narrow relapsed-non-de-escalation slice. Report it as a *qualified* extension, not a standalone driver. The L1→L2 "protective reversal" (OR 0.36, p=0.049) rests on 7 events — treat as noise.

## Null results & refuted claims (what to stop chasing)

- **Induction→maintenance "collapse to single agent" is NOT a driver (confirmed null).** Multi-drug→single-agent is *depleted* in over-splits (2/74=2.7% vs 70/706=9.9%, OR 0.25); staying multi-drug (≥3 drugs) is *enriched* (OR 2.07, p=0.005, survives line-stratified CMH). The "lenalidomide maintenance" assumption is wrong — daratumumab is the dominant maintenance single-agent (22 vs 5).
- **The four hypothesized "swing" backbone drugs are DEPLETED, not enriched (confirmed null + refuted prior).** As swing drugs: daratumumab RR 0.07 (p<1e-12), carfilzomib RR 0 (p<1e-12), pomalidomide RR 0.33, bortezomib RR 0.56; lenalidomide/dexamethasone null. Refutes the "common backbone agent drives over-splits" prior.
- **Transplant-conditioning (melphalan+autoSCT) as "the swing drug" — WEAKENED.** Real in the full sample (9/74 vs 41/706, OR 2.25, p=0.044 — reproduced, 8 patients) but fails every robustness check: leave-one-patient-out (p→0.12–0.20), Bonferroni over 35 drugs (corrected p≈1.5), and line stratification (early-line p=0.12). Suggestive, line-confounded, multiplicity-fragile — not an established driver.
- **De-escalation as an "inverted sub-curve" (zero at L2, rising) — REFUTED.** Pure x-axis artifact of COTA's inflated line: an over-split cannot stay at COTA line 2, so de-esc over-splits are definitionally pushed out of the L2 bucket. On the **doctor's line** the trend vanishes (ρ +0.556→+0.012, L2-vs-L3+ Fisher p 0.0001→1.00).
- **Line duration / inter-line gaps — mostly NULL.** Current-line duration (p=0.43), prev-line duration (p=0.23), central gap (p=0.51) all null. Only a weak near-zero-gap tail excess (gap≤1 day: 26% vs 16%, OR 1.81, p=0.049) that does NOT survive multiple-comparison correction (Bonferroni→0.54). Over-splits are not short fragments.
- **Patient-level super-spreader clustering — NULL (confirmed).** Once you condition on each patient's exposure (Binomial(n_i, p) null), per-patient counts are consistent with independence — in fact mildly *under*-dispersed (dispersion ratio 0.85; bootstrap-expected VMR 1.05 covers observed 1.28). The marginal Poisson "signal" (p=0.017) is the mis-specified trap. Transplant patient-level OR=1.00. max_lot is perfectly collinear with exposure (ρ=1.0).
- **Independent "line-burden raises rate" effect — REFUTED by endogeneity** (see Finding 4): vanishes on doctor-truth line totals.

## Caveats

- **Small n.** 74 over-splits total; many subgroup cells are single-digit (de-esc n=14 across 6 line buckets; transplant n=9/8 patients; multi→single OVER n=2). Effect-size point estimates are imprecise; treat all p-values near 0.05 as fragile.
- **Multiple comparisons across ~13 hypotheses (and many sub-tests within each).** Only **two** findings clear strict multiplicity control: identical-drug-set (p=2e-24) and PI+Steroids (the sole Bonferroni passer among 36 families). The discontinue-reason finding (p=4e-5) and the relapsed-multiphase slice (p≈5e-5) are well below any reasonable family-wise threshold; the time-gap, transplant-swing, and de-escalation-curve "signals" do not survive correction.
- **The recurring confound is COTA's own inflated line number** as an x-axis, and **identical-drug-set as an omitted variable.** Three "novel" claims (de-escalation inversion, independent line-burden, prev-multiphase) were partly or wholly explained once these were controlled. We carried the verifiers' corrected effects, not the originals. Any future per-line analysis should default to the **doctor's** line; any "regimen-structure" predictor should be co-modeled against same_set.
- **Informative missingness** for Finding 1: a blank discontinue_reason may reflect documentation sparsity rather than a true absent event — the data cannot distinguish "no event" from "uncoded event," so the causal reading is one interpretation of a robust correlation.
- **Per-row non-independence** within 136 patients is uncorrected in most tests; where checked (discontinue-reason 21 patients; multiphase patient-cluster bootstrap CI 1.58–4.90; super-spreader exposure-conditioned null) the findings held, but the team should apply patient-clustered inference before publication.
- **Parsing dependence.** Drug sets from `parsed_drugs_repaired` (ignores dose/schedule), phase counts from bracket "[" counts, transplant from regimen-string keywords. These are COTA's own representations — appropriate for diagnosing COTA's logic, but a few rows could shuffle between categories.
